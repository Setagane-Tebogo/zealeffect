About Us

Five years on the road

Mafosholo Shuttle started with a single sedan and a phone that never stopped ringing. It's grown into a fleet that covers Limpopo, Gauteng and the routes between them.

Mission

Get every passenger to their destination safely, on time, and stress-free whether it’s a long-distance journey or a special event across town.

Vision

To be the shuttle service that Limpopo businesses and travellers trust and book without a second thought, because we deliver every journey safely, reliably, and on time.

How we operate

All bookings go to one dispatch system. Our driver handles each trip and we confirm every booking by WhatsApp or Phone Call before pickup, so everything is clear and organised

Safety commitment
